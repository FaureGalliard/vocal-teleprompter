## Vocal Teleprompter 1.4.0

**Speech recognition now runs on your computer, with any microphone, and AI assistants can
write your script straight into the prompter.** This release also moves the controls
outside your text, and the window now opens right under your webcam and remembers where
you left it.

### Install

If you have 1.2.0, the app offers this update by itself: accept it and you're done.
Otherwise download `vocal-teleprompter_1.4.0_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`) and run it.

> Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer is not code-signed yet:
> **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's new

**🎤 Offline speech recognition on any microphone**
- Recognition runs locally with [Vosk](https://alphacephei.com/vosk/): no account and no
  internet while you speak. English ships with the app; any other language's model (30 to
  90 MB) is downloaded once, as soon as you pick it.
- Pick any microphone from the microphone menu, with a level meter to test it. The speech
  language starts as your system's.
- It listens for the words in your script and lifts soft voices, so it keeps up even when
  you speak quietly.

**🤖 Works with Claude and other AI assistants (MCP)**
- Ask for a presentation and it lands in the prompter, already split into slide cards and
  sized to your time. Assistants can also read the script, check its length, change
  settings and start, pause or move between slides.
- Connect Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor or VS Code in one click from
  **Settings → AI connector**. Everything stays on your computer.

**🧰 Controls that never cover the text**
- The round button in the bottom corner opens a row of controls **below** the prompter,
  outside your script. The space it opens up lets clicks through to the apps behind.
- What you use while reading stays on the bar: start/stop, pause, speech language,
  microphone, appearance and settings. Edit, paste, import, clear and the tutorial live in
  the script menu (document icon), with their names spelled out.
- Settings are grouped into **Reading**, **Window** and **General**; automatic contrast
  moved next to the colors in **Appearance**.

**🪟 A window that stays where you put it**
- The first time, it opens as a strip at the top center of the screen, right under the
  webcam. After that it reopens where and as big as you left it.
- Hiding the title bar takes just its height off the window, so the text doesn't move.

**📜 Reading**
- Choose how the script scrolls: **ahead** (the text holds still until the current line
  nears the bottom) or **fixed** (the current line stays at one height).
- At the end of a paragraph or slide, the next one scrolls into view in time.
- Click a word to continue reading from it; click it again, or between words, to edit the
  script there. No more Ctrl+click.
- A 3, 2, 1 countdown before listening starts, in the bottom row.
- Typography: font, weight, line spacing, text width and alignment.
- Words you just read stay readable, so you can glance back.

**Also**
- Rebind the global shortcuts or turn them off.
- Voice sync no longer jumps a line ahead on a repeated word.
- Slides are split by a thin line instead of a label; the bottom row already numbers them.

### Known issues

- Hiding from screen capture needs Windows 10 version 2004 or newer; on older builds the
  window shows up black instead.
- Interface translations other than English and Spanish are new; corrections are welcome.
