## Vocal Teleprompter 1.2.0

**The prompter that follows your voice is now invisible to your audience.** Share your
screen in Zoom, Meet or Teams and only you see the script. This release also brings slide
cards, pace tracking, global shortcuts, nine interface languages and automatic updates.

### Install

Download `vocal-teleprompter_1.2.0_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`) and run it. It replaces
1.1.x in place. **This is the last version you install by hand:** from now on the app
tells you when there is an update and installs it in one click.

> Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer is not code-signed yet:
> **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's new

**🕶️ Hidden from screen sharing**
- Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, OBS, Discord and screenshots leave the window out, while you
  keep seeing it. On by default; switch it off in Settings to record the app itself.

**🃏 Slide cards**
- Put a line with `---` between slides and each part becomes a card. The bottom bar shows
  *Slide 3 / 12*, and the card moves on as soon as you say its last word.

**⏱️ Pace and time**
- See how long the script takes before you start, then the elapsed time, words per
  minute and time left. Set a target duration and it tells you to speed up or slow down.

**⌨️ Global shortcuts and click-through**
- `Ctrl+Alt+Space` start/pause, `Ctrl+Alt+←/→` previous/next slide, `Ctrl+Alt+H`
  show/hide and `Ctrl+Alt+K` click-through work while Zoom or your slides have the focus.
- Click-through mode lets clicks pass through the prompter to the app behind it.

**◐ Always readable**
- Automatic contrast keeps the text readable on any background color. With a see-through
  background the text gets a halo, so it stays legible over videos or slides.
- Optional reading line keeps the current line near your camera.

**✍️ Editing**
- Write the script right in the prompter, or click any word to edit it there.
  Ctrl+click continues reading from that word.
- Settings, script, colors and microphone are remembered between sessions.

**🌍 Languages**
- Interface in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese
  and Korean, the same languages the prompter listens to.
- Japanese, Chinese, Korean and other non-Latin scripts now sync with your voice.
  They never matched before.

**Also**
- Better voice sync: it follows the nearest match using the last few spoken words, so a
  repeated word further ahead no longer makes it jump.
- The microphone picker now really switches the microphone, and shows its name.
- Settings panel, guided tour (replay it with the **?** button), keyboard shortcuts and
  lucide icons throughout.
- MIT licensed.

### Known issues

- Speech recognition uses Windows' built-in engine and needs an internet connection.
- Hiding from screen capture needs Windows 10 version 2004 or newer; on older builds the
  window shows up black instead.
- Interface translations other than English and Spanish are new; corrections are welcome.
