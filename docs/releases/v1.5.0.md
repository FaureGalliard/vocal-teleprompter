## Vocal Teleprompter 1.5.0

**Put the prompter anywhere on your slides: it reads over anything.** It looks at what is
behind its window, softens it like frosted glass, and gives every letter the color that
stands out most from what is right behind it. You never pick a color or hunt for an empty
corner again.

### Install

If you have 1.2.0 or later, the app offers this update by itself: accept it and you're
done. Otherwise download `vocal-teleprompter_1.5.0_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`) and run
it.

> Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer is not code-signed yet:
> **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's new

**🎨 Reads over anything**
- The background starts fully see-through, so your slides show behind the text.
- What shows through is softened like frosted glass. Choose how much in
  **Appearance → Soften what is behind** (20% by default).
- Every letter takes the color that reads best over what is right behind it, judged on the
  hardest spots around it, so it holds up over photos, colors and text. Within a word, a
  lone letter never stands out in another color.
- New colors fade in, and the glass cross-fades, so changes behind the window blend in.
- The window's edge is painted the same way, so it shows over anything without drawing the
  eye.
- While you edit the script, the letters keep their colors and what is behind is softened
  all the way.

**⚡ Keeps up with your slides**
- A click, a key press or a wheel turn anywhere makes it look behind the window right
  away, so a slide change is picked up in about a tenth of a second. It never sees which
  key you pressed.
- Moving the window needs no new look: it reuses its last view of the whole screen.
- All the work happens on a separate thread, so the interface stays smooth.

**🎥 Clean recordings**
- With "Hide from screen sharing" turned off, to record the prompter itself, it no longer
  blinks in OBS or other recorders: the windows behind it are asked to draw themselves
  instead of the prompter stepping out of the way.

**Also**
- Words read long ago and the other slides fade less over a see-through background, so you
  can still find your place if the prompter jumped ahead.
- GitHub Sponsors: if the app saves you time, you can now
  [support it](https://github.com/sponsors/FaureGalliard).
- Under the hood: Prettier, ESLint and Clippy on every push, and no source file over 300
  lines.

### Known issues

- A few apps draw themselves black when asked (some hardware-accelerated video, games);
  over those, with "Hide from screen sharing" off, the colors may not match what is behind.
- Hiding from screen capture needs Windows 10 version 2004 or newer.
