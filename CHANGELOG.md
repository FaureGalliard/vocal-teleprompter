# Changelog

What's new in each version of Vocal Teleprompter, latest first. The full notes are in
[docs/releases](docs/releases), and the downloads on the
[Releases](https://github.com/FaureGalliard/vocal-teleprompter/releases) page.

## 1.7.1 (2026-10-31)

Live frosted glass on Windows: a video or a window dragged behind the prompter stays soft
at once, and the letters no longer blink as they pick their colors.
[Full notes](docs/releases/v1.7.1.md)

## 1.6.1 (2026-10-30)

Linux on GNOME (Wayland), with the same glass, letters that read over anything and hiding
from screen sharing as on Windows, and a library of your scripts on every system.
[Full notes](docs/releases/v1.6.1.md)

## 1.5.0 (2026-10-29)

It reads over anything: what is behind the window is softened like frosted glass, and every
letter takes the color that stands out most from what is right behind it.
[Full notes](docs/releases/v1.5.0.md)

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-28)

Speech recognition on your computer with any microphone, AI assistants writing the script
straight into the prompter, and the controls outside the text.
[Full notes](docs/releases/v1.4.0.md)

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-26)

Invisible to your audience when you share your screen, with slide cards, pace tracking,
global shortcuts, nine interface languages and automatic updates.
[Full notes](docs/releases/v1.2.0.md)
